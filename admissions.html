Admissions, step-by-step

Five simple stages — from your first enquiry to your child's first morning at Sage.

Step 01

Inquiry & Tour

Submit an online enquiry or visit our Benin City campus for a guided tour.

Step 02

Application

Complete the official registration form and submit required documents.

Step 03

Entrance Assessment

Scheduled CBT for Primary and Secondary candidates, age-appropriate.

Step 04

Interview

A warm meeting with the Principal, parent/guardian, and the candidate.

Step 05

Enrolment

Receive your offer letter, complete fee payment, and welcome to Sage.

Admissions FAQ

The answers parents ask us most. Our admissions team is happy to clarify anything else by phone or email.

  • What documents are required at application?

    Birth certificate, two recent passport photographs, last two terms' report cards, and a transfer certificate (for transferring pupils).

  • Is there an entrance assessment?

    Yes — Primary and Secondary candidates take a Computer-Based Test (CBT) covering English, Maths, and reasoning.

  • What is the assessment fee?

    A non-refundable assessment fee of ₦10,000 is required to schedule the CBT and interview.

  • When does the school year begin?

    Our academic year follows the Nigerian calendar — first term begins in September. Mid-year admissions are considered subject to availability.

Requirements for Success

A short checklist to help you prepare a complete, confident application.

Age Eligibility

Candidates must meet the minimum age for their class — e.g. 11+ years for JSS1 entry. Nursery and Primary follow standard age bands.

Documentation

Birth certificate, two passport photographs, previous school report cards, and a transfer certificate (for transferring pupils).

Assessment Fee

A non-refundable assessment fee of ₦10,000 is required to schedule the entrance CBT and family interview.

Please note: The ₦10,000 assessment fee is payable at the point of scheduling the CBT and is non-refundable.
