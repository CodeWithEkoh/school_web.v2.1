About Sage Scholar

Heritage, mission, and the people shaping our scholars.

Our History

Founded in Benin City in 2008, Sage Scholar Academy began as a small Cambridge-aligned primary school with a single conviction: that Nigerian children deserve an education that is both globally rigorous and locally rooted.

Over the years we have grown into a full Nursery to Senior Secondary institution serving families across Edo State, with alumni studying at top universities in Nigeria, the UK, and North America. Our journey is shaped by an enduring commitment to scholarship, character, and service.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver a world-class, character-first education that prepares Nigerian students to lead, create, and serve with confidence in any room they walk into.

Our Vision

To be West Africa's most trusted K-12 academy — known for academic excellence, ethical leadership, and graduates who shape industries and communities.

A Message from the Principal

"At Sage Scholar Academy, we do not simply prepare students for examinations — we prepare them for life. Every classroom, every mentor relationship, and every co-curricular activity is designed to nurture courage, curiosity, and conscience."

We are honoured that you are considering us for your child's educational journey, and we look forward to walking that journey with your family.

Dr. Ada Okoye
Principal · Cambridge Ed.D.
